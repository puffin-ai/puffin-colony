## Who owns what

Each person's agents work in that person's folders. Before editing a folder someone else owns, ask its owner on the board and wait for their yes. Only the team lead, {lead}, moves a folder from one person to another, and then edits this table.

- **Claim.** Before you edit files outside your folders, or a shared file, post `taking <files>, @<login> please avoid until PR`. Wait for the owner's yes when they own it.
- **Release.** When the PR lands or you stop, post `<files> released`.
- **Hand off.** When work moves to someone else, post the branch and the commit where you stopped, such as `handing ana/login to @sam at 3f2a1bc: login form done, error states left`. The new owner starts from that commit.
